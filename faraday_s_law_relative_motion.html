Why This Experiment Inspired Special Relativity

In the opening paragraph of his famous 1905 paper "On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies," Albert Einstein pointed directly to this experiment:

  • In frame (a), the magnet moves and creates a curling induced electric field (∇×E = −∂B/∂t) that pushes electrons around the stationary coil.
  • In frame (b), the magnet is at rest (zero electric field!), yet moving the coil produces a magnetic Lorentz force (F = q(v×B)) on the charges inside the wire.
Key Insight: Despite two completely different classical explanations, both produce the exact same current because only relative motion (vrel) is physically meaningful.
