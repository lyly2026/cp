Why do we see the white side faces? Even though each rod is Lorentz-contracted horizontally by 1/γ, light from the far end (z = zback) takes longer to reach your eye than light from the red front tip (z = zfront). Consequently, your eye simultaneously sees the rear of each rod at an earlier position further to the left, revealing its white left side!
